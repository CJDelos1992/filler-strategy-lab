MATHEMATICAL MODEL · 03

A game of connected components.

Each cell is a graph vertex. Horizontal and vertical neighbors define the edges. A player's territory grows by flooding adjacent neutral vertices of a chosen color, including any connected same-color chain.

01 / STATE

Bitboard representation

Two integer masks track owned cells, six masks store the original colors, and bit shifts generate adjacency. Every move preserves the opponent's territory.

s = (S₁, S₂, c₁, c₂, turn, stalls)
02 / EVALUATION

Territory heuristic

D is owned tile difference, F is frontier difference, R is obstacle-aware nearest-territory difference, M is immediate capture potential difference. Positive favors P1.

H(s) = 10D + 2F + 4R + M
03 / SEARCH

Adversarial lookahead

Minimax alternates max and min nodes. Alpha–beta pruning discards branches that cannot improve the decision. Terminal wins dominate heuristic estimates.

V(s) = max/min V(T(s, a))
Rules and implementation assumptions

Players start at opposite corners, each owning one tile. On a turn, choose one of the four colors different from both current player colors. All connected, orthogonally adjacent neutral tiles of that color are captured; owned tiles cannot be stolen. Unclaimed tiles retain their initial colors. The match normally ends when all tiles are occupied, and the larger territory wins.

Zero-gain moves are permitted; to guarantee finite automated experiments, this independent implementation declares a draw after 12 consecutive zero-gain moves, even if the raw tile counts differ. Seeded boards avoid identical neighboring colors and identical starting-corner colors; imported boards may contain adjacent identical colors. The app's exact move restrictions and board generator have not been independently verified against GamePigeon. This project is not affiliated with GamePigeon.

Search is depth-limited, not a proof of optimal play. Mobility is evaluated symmetrically under the currently legal colors, not a forecast of the next turn's legal colors. Benchmarks describe these strategies under these rules and board distribution only.
